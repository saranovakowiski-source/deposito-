yeah, 2026, world cup fever, north america... so, there's nothin better than talkin abt soccer. wb brazilian soccer? hellyeaaaah! sooo... brazil and jamaica is such a random duo and we know it. but do u know why? their affinity began long before Jamaica's modern tournament successes. in 1964, a Kingston football club explicitly named itself Santos after Pelé’s legendary Brazilian side, adopting their iconic colors. the bond became physical and electric on January 31, 1971, when Pelé and Santos arrived in Kingston to play at the National Stadium, drawing a massive crowd of 30,000 people and receiving the key to the city. this admiration was further bridged by Jamaican football icon Allan 'Skill' Cole, who not only scored against Santos in that 1971 visit but later went on to play professionally in Brazil for Náutico in 1972.
